## Full description

Wake up to the news, read aloud.

Hark Daily gathers the stories you care about from trusted sources (CBC News, TechCrunch, BetaKit) and turns them into a concise audio briefing, ready before you get up. Press play over coffee, on the commute or on a run, and hear the day's news in minutes.

**Your news, your way**
• Curated sections: Top Stories, AI, Tech, Politics, Entertainment, Science, and Sports
• Dynamic neural voices: C-3PO, Alice, Jerry, and Mike
• Dual listening modes: Full briefing with section intros or swift Rapid headline flow
• Reorder categories to prioritize what you care about first

**Listen and follow along**
• Natural voices read each story with time-adaptive greetings
• Each story card highlights and auto-scrolls as it's read; tap any card to seek
• Skip, scrub and change the playback speed
• Every story links directly to the full article from its publisher

**Private by design**
• No ads, no commercial tracking pixels, no algorithmic feeds
• Signing in is optional and only syncs your preferences

**Smarter summaries, if you want them**
• Works out of the box with built-in summaries
• Add a free key from Groq, Gemini or OpenRouter and each story is rewritten for listening
• Spot a summary that's wrong? Report it right from the story

Free. A new five-minute brief is recorded every morning, ready before you wake.

## Data safety answers

| Data | Collected | Why | Shared |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email address | Only if you sign in | Account and settings sync | No |
| App settings and sources | Only if you sign in | Sync across devices | No |
| City for weather | Typed by you, sent to the weather service | Forecast | No |
| Summary reports | Only when you report a summary: the story, your reason and an optional note | Fixing summaries | No |
| Story text | Sent only to the AI service you pick (Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI or your own), and only if you add your own key | Summaries | No |

No location permission, no advertising ID, no analytics. Data is encrypted in transit. Users can delete their
account in Settings.
