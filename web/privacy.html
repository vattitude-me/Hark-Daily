Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Hark Daily turns curated and custom news into a short, focused spoken briefing. It collects as little as it can to do that. There are zero commercial ads. We use Google Analytics to understand live aggregate usage, search topics, and audio playback engagement so we can continuously improve the briefing experience. Nothing is ever sold, leased, or shared with marketers.

What's stored with your account

You don't need an account to use the Android app or listen to the daily showcase on the web. If you sign in (on the web, or in the app to sync), we store:

  • Your sign-in: your email address, and your name and profile picture if you use Google Sign-In.
  • Your settings: the name you're greeted by, your weather city and its coordinates, your chosen narrator (e.g. C-3PO, Alice), briefing mode (Full or Rapid), and how many stories you want per topic.
  • Your news links: any custom feeds, sections, or source priorities you configure.
  • On the web: your daily briefing cache and web push notification subscriptions if you opt into morning alerts.

This is kept securely in Supabase, and each account can only read and modify its own data.

What stays on your phone

The Android app stores your preferences, downloaded offline briefings, on-device voice models, and any optional personal AI API keys directly on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the app completely removes them.

Who else sees what

  • News sources (CBC News, TechCrunch, BetaKit, and custom feeds) receive ordinary HTTP GET requests when our pipeline reads public headlines and summary articles.
  • Open-Meteo receives city coordinates to return temperature and forecast conditions. No personal identifiers are sent.
  • Summarization & Voice AI: Daily news scripts are summarized via Groq or Google Gemini API. Spoken briefings are synthesized once globally on our private cloud voice server running Chatterbox-Turbo / Kokoro. Audio clips are saved to static storage and shared across all listeners. Individual listeners never have their identity or playback activity sent to voice models.
  • Google Analytics: Measures aggregate website traffic, search queries, and audio playback engagement on the web app (measurement ID G-8LV7D5TDT2) to help us improve performance and feature relevance. Analytics data is anonymized and never sold.
  • Google handles authentication when you explicitly click "Continue with Google".
  • GitHub hosts public release APKs and source code repositories.

Reporting a summary

If you report a story's summary in the app, we receive the story (headline, summary, link and source), the reason you picked, any note you write, the app version and, if you're signed in, your account. We use it only to fix summaries, and reports are deleted once they're reviewed or with your account.

How long it's kept

Web briefings are deleted after a few days. Everything else is kept until you change it or delete your account.

Deleting your account

Choose Delete account in Settings, on the web or in the Android app. Your sign-in, settings, links, briefings and audio are permanently removed, usually within a minute. Briefings stored on your phone stay there until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

Hark Daily isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
